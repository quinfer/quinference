I help students prepare for a future shaped by data and technology, teaching them to experiment, to read evidence carefully, and to make decisions they can stand over. I want them to finish their courses able to do the work, but also curious, honest about what they don't yet know, and willing to ask hard questions.

As Director of the Centre for Finance and Responsible Technology at Ulster University Business School, I work with industry and policy partners on practical problems in markets, risk, and compliance. Current collaborations include doctoral projects with Funds Axis, Pytillia, and Napier AI, supported by the Department of the Economy.

My own research sits at the meeting point of finance, statistical inference, and responsible machine learning, looking at markets, risk, and how algorithms behave when they meet regulation. I try to do it the way I teach it: assumptions made explicit, uncertainty taken seriously, and claims kept proportionate to what the evidence will bear.
